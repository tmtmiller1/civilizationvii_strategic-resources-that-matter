[h1]Strategic Resources That Matter[/h1]
[b]Version 1.0.1. Compatible with Civilization VII 1.5.0. Single-player only[/b]

Horses, Iron, Niter, Hardwood, Oil, Coal and Rubber count for the units that need them. In the base game a missing one costs you nothing; with the mod, a unit whose owner has none of its resource fights weaker and trains slower, losing your last one brings the penalty back a turn later, and the count keeps adding strength past six. Nothing is forbidden unless you ask for it: set Unsupplied Units to one of its two locked values and a unit cannot be trained or bought at all while you have none of its resource.

Four options in Advanced Options, under Game Settings, so you choose the rules per game: the penalty from Off up to a lock, the bonus past six, a second resource for every Modern unit (off by default, because it's hard), and the Samurai's free tier-up. The AI plays under the same rules; independent powers and city-states are never affected.

[h2]What changes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Unsupplied units.[/b] A unit whose owner has none of its strategic resource loses Combat Strength on each strength it has (melee, and ranged and bombard where it has them), and its city puts less Production toward it. Default: -3 and a third less Production. One of the resource lifts the penalty; the game's own +1 for each applies as before, up to six.
[*][b]Losing a resource hurts.[/b] The game notices a resource arriving but not one going away. The mod has it check every player again each turn, so a resource lost to a raid, a lost settlement or an ended trade route brings the penalty back a turn later, for you and for the AI alike.
[*][b]Past six.[/b] Where the game's own bonus stops, the count keeps adding: +1 more Combat Strength at 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18 and 20 of a resource.
[*][b]Locked units (the two hardest values).[/b] Set Unsupplied Units to "Locked: training" or "Locked: training and upgrade" and a unit that needs a strategic resource cannot be trained or bought while you have none of it, on top of the Harsh penalty in the field. It stays in the production list, greyed out, with a "Locked: no Iron" pill, and is locked again the moment your last one goes. Under the second value, no unit can be upgraded into it either, and a commander's Upgrade All greys out with the reason. The Warrior line, the Scout, ranged infantry, the Catapult and Trebuchet, the Cog and Modern infantry need nothing, so everyone can always defend. A unit that needs nothing stays in your list until you hold the resource for the next one in its line, so Gunpowder never leaves you without a siege unit, and the upgrade still works once you have it. The AI is locked by the same rule and never sticks on a locked unit.
[*][b]Modern Supply Chains (off by default).[/b] Every Modern unit that needs a resource needs a second one: artillery Niter and Coal, armour and battleships Oil and Coal, light warships Coal and Niter, aircraft Rubber and Oil (Iron isn't on Modern maps, so Coal stands for steel). Each missing resource costs the full penalty, and under a locked value the unit needs both to be trained. It's a hard game.
[/list]

[h2]Game setup options[/h2]
Play Now uses the defaults. The options are fixed for the whole game once it starts.
[list]
[*][b]Unsupplied Units:[/b] Off, Light (-2, 20% less Production), Standard (-3, 33% less), Harsh (-5, 50% less), Locked: training, Locked: training and upgrade. Default Standard.
[*][b]Strategic Resources Past Six:[/b] Off or On. Default On.
[*][b]Modern Supply Chains:[/b] Off or On. Default Off.
[*][b]Free Tier-Up by Kills:[/b] Off or On. Default On.
[/list]

[h2]What the game tells you[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Unit panel.[/b] A unit short of its resource gains one more cell in its stats, the resource's icon over the penalty in red, with "Unsupplied: no Horses (-3 Combat Strength, slower to train)" as its tooltip.
[*][b]Production list.[/b] A pill like the game's own "Ageless" on each unit that needs a resource: "Unsupplied: no Niter" on a red tint while you have none, "Niter: 2" once you have some, "Locked: no Iron" under Gated Units.
[*][b]Combat preview.[/b] The penalty as a line of its own, for both sides ("-3 Unsupplied: no Niter"), and the strength past six beside the game's own ("+1 Niter: 8 or more").
[*][b]Resources.[/b] Each strategic resource's description names the units it supplies for you in this age.
[*][b]Notification.[/b] When you lose the last of a resource your units need: "Supply lost: no Niter. Your Field Cannon and 3 other units fight at -3 and train slower until you get Niter back." Once per loss; it never holds the turn up.
[*][b]First time.[/b] An advisor callout the first time you select a unit short of its resource, and a second one under Gated Units for the lock. Each shows once, and only with tutorials or advisor warnings on.
[*][b]Civilopedia.[/b] A page under Game Concepts, Combat, and a line on each such unit's page.
[/list]

[h2]Known limits[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Single-player.[/b] In multiplayer, losing a resource does not bring the penalty back until that player gains a resource.
[*][b]New games.[/b] A game started with the mod needs it to load; added to a game under way, it takes effect from the next new game.
[*][b]Under a locked value,[/b] a unit already in the production queue when the resource is lost keeps its progress and is completed.
[*][b]Another mod that sets the game's resource minimums itself[/b] keeps the engine's rule by its own numbers; this mod's lock texts then step back to their plain form.
[/list]
