[b]Version 1.0.0 · 11 October 2026[/b]
Horses, Iron, Niter, Oil, Coal and Rubber matter again: going without one costs the units that need it.

[b]Added[/b]
• Unsupplied units. A unit that draws on a strategic resource its owner holds no copy of fights weaker and trains slower: -3 Combat Strength and a third less Production toward it by default. One copy lifts the penalty, and the game's own +1 per copy applies as before. Melee strength always; ranged and bombard strength only on units that have them. Cavalry need Horses; infantry from the second tier need Iron; Exploration siege and ships of the line need Niter; Modern artillery Niter, armour and battleships Oil, light warships Coal, aircraft Rubber. Unique units need what the unit they replace needs. Modern infantry need nothing.
• Losing a resource hurts. The game notices a resource arriving but not one going away; the mod has it check every player again at the start of each turn, so a resource lost to a raid or a lost settlement brings the penalty back a turn later, for the AI players too.
• Past six copies, where the game's own bonus stops, units gain +1 more Combat Strength at 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18 and 20 copies.
• Two game setup options: Unsupplied Units (Off, Light, Standard, Harsh; Standard by default) and Strategic Resources Past Six (Off, On; On by default).
• The unit panel says when a unit is unsupplied, for example “Unsupplied: no Iron (-3 Combat Strength, slower to train)”.
• Text in all twelve of the game's languages.
• Carries the Tower Settings Keeper, so every mod's settings survive a restart.
Single-player. In multiplayer, losing a resource does not bring the penalty back until that player gains a resource.
