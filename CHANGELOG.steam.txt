[b]Version 1.0.0 · 11 October 2026[/b]
Horses, Iron, Niter, Oil, Coal and Rubber matter again: going without one costs the units that need it.

[b]Added[/b]
• Unsupplied units. A unit that draws on a strategic resource its owner holds no copy of fights weaker and trains slower: -3 Combat Strength and a third less Production toward it by default. One copy lifts the penalty, and the game's own +1 per copy applies as before. Melee strength always; ranged and bombard strength only on units that have them. Cavalry need Horses; infantry from the second tier need Iron; Exploration siege and heavy warships from the second tier need Niter; Modern artillery Niter, armour and battleships Oil, light warships Coal, aircraft Rubber. Unique units need what the unit they replace needs. Modern infantry need nothing. A copy that reaches a player over a trade route counts the same as a mined one, from the turn the route lands.
• Losing a resource hurts. The game notices a resource arriving but not one going away; the mod has it check every player again at the start of each turn, so a resource lost to a raid or a lost settlement brings the penalty back a turn later, for the AI players too.
• Past six copies, where the game's own bonus stops, units gain +1 more Combat Strength at 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18 and 20 copies.
• Two game setup options: Unsupplied Units (Off, Light, Standard, Harsh; Standard by default) and Strategic Resources Past Six (Off, On; On by default).
• The unit panel says when a unit is unsupplied, for example “Unsupplied: no Iron (-3 Combat Strength, slower to train)”.
• The combat preview names the penalty as a line of its own, for both sides, for example “-3 Unsupplied: no Niter”, and the strength past six copies as one line beside the game's own, for example “+1 Niter: 8 or more copies”.
• A Civilopedia page under Game Concepts, Combat: the setup values, what counts as a copy, losing one, past six copies, and which units need what. Each unit that needs a resource says so on its own page, with the penalty the game was set up with.
• Text in all twelve of the game's languages.
• Carries the Tower Settings Keeper, so every mod's settings survive a restart.
Single-player. In multiplayer, losing a resource does not bring the penalty back until that player gains a resource.
